The range

Six departments.
Twelve categories.

The heart of the RAC catalogue is cooking — cookers, built-in ovens and hobs — with refrigeration, television, small appliances and fitness around it. Take the full line, or enter with one department and widen it later.

The range is shown at category level. Full model lists, specifications and packing codes are sent with the dealer pack.

How the catalogue is built

Three tiers
inside every category.

Every category is built on three tiers, so one brand answers three kinds of customer instead of losing the sale to the shop next door.

  • ESSENTIAL — the entry model at a competitive price that opens the sale.
  • PLUS — the most-asked-for specification at the best value point; the volume backbone.
  • PRO — inverter, higher efficiency and upper specification for the customer who wants the best.
