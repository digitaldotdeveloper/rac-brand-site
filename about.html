About RAC

Appliances for real
homes, not for
showroom photos.

RAC started from a simple observation: a family wants a machine that runs for years at a price it can actually pay, and a trader wants a brand that turns over quickly without drowning them in service calls. Most brands solve one of those. The RAC line was built to solve both.

So we concentrate on what actually matters: dependable core components, specifications suited to our grid and our climate, parts that stay available, and pricing that does not move every week.

What we hold to

Three commitments
we don't trade away.

An appliance brand is built on trust far more than on advertising. These are the points we expect to be judged on.

It works, and keeps working

We choose the compressor, the motor and the control board before we choose how the unit looks. Looks sell it once; performance sells the next one.

An honest price

We don't inflate a price so a discount can be announced later. A clear dealer price and a clear retail price — and both stay put.

The trader is never left alone

An after-sales problem is ours. The technician, the part and the warranty come from us, so your relationship with your customer stays intact.

Service & warranty

A warranty only means
something when the
part is on the shelf.

Every RAC unit carries a 12-month warranty, with an extended term on compressors in cooling products. More importantly, spare parts are held locally and guaranteed available for five years after a model is last sold.

  • A central workshop and authorised technicians
  • A bilingual warranty card inside every box
  • Dealer parts requests dispatched within a 48-hour target
  • Fault-rate reporting shared with dealers, not hidden from them
