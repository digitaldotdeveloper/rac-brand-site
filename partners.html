Partnership levels

Three ways
to work with us.

Pick the level that matches your size today and move up as volume grows. Terms and support grow with you.

Level 01

Retail point

A retail shop carrying a selected RAC assortment. Wholesale pricing, signage and full warranty backing.

  • Open orders from local stock
  • Signage and a display shelf
  • No territory commitment

Level 02

Authorised dealer

The core partner in an area. Better tiers, territory protection and a share of the marketing budget.

  • A protected, named territory
  • Agreed payment terms
  • Sales-team training

Level 03

Wholesale distributor

Container programmes and re-distribution to a network of shops. Annual programme pricing and full logistics support.

  • Annual container programme
  • Best available tier
  • Support extended to your own shops

Final terms are set after we review the territory and the volume. We do not sign more than one authorised dealer per area.

What we look for

We're not looking for
the biggest — we're
looking for the serious.

A small shop that looks after its customers and pays on time matters more to us than a big name that buys once and disappears.

  • An existing business in appliances or electronics
  • Display space or suitable warehousing
  • The ability to display and sell, not just to store
  • Commitment to the retail price policy
