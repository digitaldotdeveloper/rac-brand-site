What every trader asks

Two questions.
Two straight answers.

Any trader asks two things before taking on a brand: what turns over fastest, and what won't come back to me as a problem. Here are both answers, from our own numbers.

Fastest moving

  1. 01

    Gas cookers

    The backbone. A cooker is the first thing a new household buys and the last thing it postpones.

  2. 02

    Small appliances

    Repeat business at an easy price — vacuums, blenders and countertop ovens that send a customer out with a bag.

  3. 03

    Water dispensers

    Both seasonal and year-round — homes, offices and shops, in top-load, bottom-load and tea-bar.

Fewest service calls

  1. 01

    Televisions

    They effectively don't come back. Sold, installed, and never heard from again — the best thing that can be said about an appliance.

  2. 02

    Top-load dispensers

    A simple mechanism and next to no faults. The customer doesn't call to complain — they call to buy a second one for the office.

A unit that never comes back saves you the technician's time and your name in front of the customer — profit that never shows on the invoice.

Quality

Engineered for
the power we
actually have.

Appliances designed for stable grids struggle in our markets. RAC units are specified against voltage swings, high ambient heat and repeated outages — because that is the reality they will run in.

  • Tropical-rated (T-class) compressors on cooling products
